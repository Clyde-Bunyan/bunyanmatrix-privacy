Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Introduction

Bunyan Matrix respects user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in connection with Horizon Job Search ("Horizon"), a job-search and application-management tool operated by Bunyan Matrix.

About Horizon

Horizon helps users discover job opportunities, evaluate job suitability, manage applications, organize CVs, and track recruitment-related communications.

Horizon is currently designed primarily as a local application running on the user's own computer.

Information Horizon May Process

Depending on features enabled by the user, Horizon may process information such as:

  • job listings and job descriptions
  • employer and company information
  • application status and application history
  • CV and professional profile information
  • recruitment-related email information
  • job-source information
  • job-search preferences
  • information explicitly authorized through supported third-party integrations

LinkedIn Information

If Horizon integrates with LinkedIn, Horizon will only access LinkedIn information through officially authorized LinkedIn APIs, products, or user-approved workflows.

Horizon will not intentionally use unauthorized scraping, automated profile extraction, or private LinkedIn APIs.

Any LinkedIn information accessed through an approved integration will be used only for functionality authorized by the user and permitted by LinkedIn.

Horizon does not sell LinkedIn data.

Third-Party Services

Horizon may interact with third-party services such as job boards, recruitment platforms, email providers, or other employment-related services.

Each third-party service operates under its own privacy policy and terms. Horizon does not control those third-party privacy practices.

Local Data Storage

Horizon is designed to store much of its application data locally on the user's computer.

Where credentials or access tokens are required, Horizon is designed to use secure credential-storage mechanisms where supported.

Users remain responsible for the security of their own devices and accounts.

Data Sharing

Bunyan Matrix does not sell personal information collected through Horizon.

Information is not shared with third parties except where:

  • required to provide a user-requested integration
  • explicitly authorized by the user
  • required by law
  • necessary to protect security or integrity

Data Retention and Deletion

Users may remove locally stored Horizon information using available application controls or by removing the application's local data.

Access granted to third-party services may also be revoked through the relevant third-party service where supported.

If a future hosted version of Horizon stores information on Bunyan Matrix systems, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Security

Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect information handled by Horizon.

No software or internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Horizon develops, new integrations are added, or legal/platform requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page.
